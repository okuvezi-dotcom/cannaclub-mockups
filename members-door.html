Member app · Design mockup

The members' door

A member cannot see a single product until four separate things are true — and once inside, every order passes three more checks before anyone is paid or anything moves. Walk it end to end on the phone. The rail names what the server verifies at each step.

Act I — Getting in

Built Phases 1 and 2a. Enforced server-side today and covered by the test suite.
  1. 1

    Prove the phone

    A code goes to the number. The account is created on redemption, not before — a phone number is not trustworthy until something sent to it comes back.

    Single use, 10-minute expiry, 5 attempts, 3 sends per 15 minutes
  2. 2

    Prove the age

    Consent is captured for this purpose alone before the document goes anywhere. The age bar comes from the verified document, never a self-reported field.

    Checksum, date of birth, 18 or over, one ID number per account
  3. 3

    Choose a club

    Clubs awaiting or refused platform vetting are absent from the list, not greyed out. You see that a club exists; you see nothing it holds.

    Only clubs cleared to trade are listed, and only to a signed-in member
  4. 4

    Accept the club's terms

    The exact wording accepted is fingerprinted and kept against the membership. Amend the terms mid-application and the stale acceptance is refused, not quietly recorded.

    Version re-checked server-side; hash retained as evidence
  5. 5

    The club decides

    Not the platform. And it cannot admit anyone who has not signed — an approved membership without a signature would be a customer, not a member.

    Only that club's own staff may decide; verification re-checked at approval

Act II — Shopping

Design intent Phases 2b, 3 and 4. Designed and specified; not yet written. This is what the next tranche buys.
  1. 6

    Browse behind the gate

    Search, filters, stock levels and tiles like any storefront — except every query passes the membership gate first, so a new screen cannot accidentally skip it. Lapse the membership and the shop closes with no job having to run.

    Verified adult + club trading + live membership, re-evaluated per request
  2. 7

    Stay inside the limit

    A rolling personal allowance, counted per member across the window rather than per basket. Fill the cart past it and checkout is refused, not warned about. Accessories do not count.

    Rolling-window possession ledger; blocked at checkout, not at the button
  3. 8

    Deliver inside the zone

    The address is tested against the club's own delivery polygon in the database, not a radius or a postcode list. Watch the map refuse an address outside it — there is no order to place.

    Geometric containment against the club's active zone; admin exclusions override
  4. 9

    Pay by bank

    Card networks will not process this, so payment runs on bank rails. The club cannot see the order at all until the provider confirms the money — it is invisible, not pending.

    Confirmed by signed webhook, never by the client; unpaid orders expire
  5. 10

    Check ID at the door

    Live tracking to the door, then the driver verifies the person receiving is the member who ordered. An order cannot reach delivered without that check recorded.

    Delivered is reachable only from ID-verified; proof of delivery captured

Membership agreement

This is the club's own agreement, not the platform's. Read it before accepting.

Version 3 · issued 2026-06-01

1. What this club is

A private, closed association of adult members. Nothing held by the club is offered to the public, and membership cannot be transferred or shared.

2. Your undertakings

You are over 18 and have verified your identity. You will not resell, supply or hand on anything obtained through the club.

3. Quantities

The club applies a rolling personal allowance. Reaching it suspends further collection until the window passes.

4. Ending membership

You may leave at any time. The club may end your membership for breach, which closes your access to its catalogue immediately.

5. Your information

The club receives your name, membership number and delivery address. It never receives your ID number.

    About this mockup

    An unbuilt interface, drawn to show intended design. Nothing reaches a server. Every club, strain, price, rating, address, slot and driver is invented. The ID-number and sign-in rules are the real ones from the codebase. Each screen names its phase and whether that phase is built.

    What is real here: the ID number checksum, date-of-birth extraction and 18+ test; the six-digit code with its expiry, five-attempt cap and send throttle; the order of both acts and what each step checks. These mirror the rules in the codebase as at 23 August 2026.

    What is not: every club, strain, price, rating, review, address, delivery slot and driver, all invented for this page. Act I is built and tested. Act II — the shop, the possession ledger, delivery zones, payments and the driver handoff — is designed and specified but not written, and each of its screens says so in the strip beneath it. Bank-rail providers are named in the architecture decision records; none is integrated.
